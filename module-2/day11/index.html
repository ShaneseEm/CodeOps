About Habesha Market

Habesha Market is an online marketplace dedicated to bringing authentic, high-quality Ethiopian food products and handcrafted items to your doorstep.

Our mission is to celebrate Ethiopian culture and empower local farmers and producers by connecting them with customers around the globe. Every product is selected with care to ensure genuine taste, quality, and sustainability.
